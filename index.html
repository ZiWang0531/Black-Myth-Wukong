PROJECT NAME

Black Myth:
Wukong

Researching cultural adaptation strategies in game localization using Andre Lefevere's Rewriting Theory.

View Final Thesis

Overview

This project focuses on the localization of cultural elements, mythological references, and character names in the globally acclaimed game "Black Myth: Wukong". The aim is to propose strategies that successfully bridge the gap between Eastern mythology and Western audiences.

Comparative Analysis

Conducted extensive text analysis of Chinese and English versions, focusing on narrative texts, skill descriptions, and character introductions.

Cultural Adjustment

Collaborated with localization experts to explore ideological and cultural adjustments tailored for Western audiences without losing the original essence.

Strategic Goals

Balancing cultural retention and Western market preferences.

Cultural Retention

Preserving the core essence of Chinese mythology, ensuring symbols and character identities remain intact.

Narrative Resonance

Translating skill descriptions and lore in a way that resonates emotionally and contextually with Western players.

Market Viability

Proposing localization adjustments that directly cater to the gaming habits and reading preferences of the target market.
